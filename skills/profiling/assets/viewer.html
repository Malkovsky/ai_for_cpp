Capture details

Capture scope is supplied by the workload; a graph alone cannot establish that setup was excluded. Inline ancestry comes from DWARF. Only duplicate unwind wrappers are collapsed; recursive calls and unknown sampled frames are retained.

A load stall can be attributed to a consuming instruction. A wide popcount frame therefore does not establish that popcount computation itself is expensive. Horizontal position is not chronological time.
